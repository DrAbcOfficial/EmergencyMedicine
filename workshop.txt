description=[*][b]Ten drugs[/b] — morphine, naloxone, fentanyl, oxycontin, sufentanil, methamphetamine, tranexamic acid, sulfadimidine, dexmedetomidine, dextromethorphan. Heal, reverse an overdose, stop the bleeding, fight infection, sedate livestock, silence a cough.
description=[*][b]Addiction & withdrawal[/b] — repeated opioid injections raise your addiction baseline; quit, and withdrawal sets in gradually, a full-screen haze swallowing your vision until the next dose or a long, brutal cold-turkey detox ends it. Amphetamine trades brief wakefulness and stamina for a long crash; when both withdrawals hit at once, your life drains straight away.
description=[*][b]Effects & side effects[/b] — drug headaches, drug fevers, somnolence, shoddy sedation, plus a fentanyl patch on the right upper arm steadily dispensing pain relief — and veterinary dexmedetomidine is a dice roll: 60% chance of calming down, 40% worse than doing nothing.
description=[*][b]Wound care[/b] — cauterize lacerations with a lighter, dig bullets out with bare hands, cut out a bite before the virus takes hold, close deep wounds with glue and a stapler, build a book splint from two books and rags, immobilize a fracture with tape and a stick, craft your own grass bandages.
description=[*][b]The auto-injector watch[/b] — load one ampoule and carry it worn. The moment your health drops below a quarter, it presses the needle for you.
description=[/list]
